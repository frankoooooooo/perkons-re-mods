# perkons-re-mods (frankoooooooo)

Sequencer mods for the Erica Synths Pērkons HD-01, built with [perkons-re](https://github.com/kiteless/perkons-re). They are unofficial, and not affiliated with Erica Synths.

| mod | status | |
|---|---|---|
| [microtiming](microtiming) | **tested on hardware** ([results](microtiming/TESTING.md)) | Hold a step and turn TEMPO to play it up to 5/6 of a step early or late |

No firmware files are included here. You build your own image from the official firmware with the Mod Matrix. Modified firmware is used at your own risk.
